Edge Impulse Studio #1061475 · 2026

ESP32-S3 Voice-Controlled LEDs using Edge Impulse

An end-to-end real-time TinyML keyword-spotting system deployed directly on an ESP32-S3 N16R8 microcontroller. Audio captured via an INMP441 I2S digital microphone is preprocessed with Mel-Frequency Energy (MFE) and fed into an INT8 quantized MobileNetV1 neural network. Successfully classifies spoken commands ("one", "two", "three") with 95.5% accuracy and drives GPIO LEDs in real time completely on-device without internet or cloud latency.

ESP32-S3 Voice-Controlled LEDs — Deep Dive

Edge Impulse Project ID: #1061475 (KWS_NUM) · Target: ESP32-S3 N16R8

🔌 Hardware Wiring (I2S Microphone & LEDs)

The INMP441 digital microphone captures audio over I2S mono at 16 kHz:

"one"GPIO 12LED 1 Toggle / On

📊 Dataset & Signal Preprocessing

  • Dataset: Google Speech Commands (11,570 samples, ~3 hours 10 mins of audio).
  • Sampling Frequency: 16,000 Hz, Window Size: 1,000 ms (stride: 500 ms).
  • Preprocessing Block: Audio (MFE) — Mel-Frequency Energy filterbank extracting spectrogram features for speech representation.

🧠 Model Architecture & Quantization

  • Base Network: MobileNetV1 (Width Multiplier: 0.1).
  • Classifier Head: 128 Dense neurons with Dropout (0.1) & 3 Output classes (one, two, three).
  • Quantization: INT8 quantized via TFLite Micro for low memory footprint and high execution speed.
